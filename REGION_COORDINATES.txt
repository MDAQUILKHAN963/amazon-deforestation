DEFORESTATION DETECTOR — TEST COORDINATES
=========================================
The app has two regions. Enter a coordinate INSIDE one of these boxes.


HASDEO (INDIA)   — use POSITIVE numbers
------------------------------------------------------
  Latitude  : 22.30  to  23.00   (North)
  Longitude : 82.40  to  82.90   (East)
  Location  : Chhattisgarh, India (Korba / Hasdeo coalfield)

  Try these (Region = Hasdeo (India), Year = 2023):
    Deforestation:  lat 22.341, lon 82.607
    Deforestation:  lat 22.688, lon 82.579
    Forest (clear): lat 22.450, lon 82.700


AMAZON (BRAZIL)  — use NEGATIVE numbers
------------------------------------------------------
  Latitude  : -4.39  to  -3.33   (South)
  Longitude : -55.20 to  -54.48  (West)
  Location  : Brazilian Amazon, Para state (near the Tapajos river)

  Try this (Region = Amazon (Brazil), Year = 2021):
    lat -4.05, lon -54.90


HOW TO TEST
------------------------------------------------------
1. Pick the Region in the dropdown.
2. Enter a Latitude and Longitude from that region's range above.
3. Choose a Year and click "Analyze".
4. You will see: Satellite image | Actual deforestation | Model prediction.
   Red = deforested area.

TIP: Test one deforestation spot AND one forest spot — this shows the
model actually tells them apart, not just predicts "red" everywhere.

NOTE: Outside these two boxes the model was not trained, so results
there are not reliable.
